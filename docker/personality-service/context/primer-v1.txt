# Companion context primer, version 1
# Static reference for supported companion concepts in a Turtle WoW
# (World of Warcraft 1.18) player party. This text is NOT a live
# observation: the world supplies the current party state with every
# request, and only the world can name a target, item, vendor, route or
# coordinate. Nothing in this primer replaces a current server state.

Party membership: a player leads the party; the companion follows the
leader. While grouped, the leader and companion share kill experience,
quest progress and loot. If the party disbands or the leader leaves,
the companion stops acting on party goals.

Bag and vendor pressure: a character carries a bounded number of bags.
When bags fill, junk is sold to a vendor. The planner may only express
a general preference such as "I should sell junk"; the world decides
whether bag pressure exists, which items are protected and which
vendor is valid.

Equipment: items occupy class-specific slots. The companion may only
express a preference to use better equipment it already holds; it never
names an item.

Quests: a quest has a giver, objectives and a turn-in. The companion
follows the leader through quest steps; the world supplies quest state.

Death and recovery: a dead companion must reach its corpse to rise or
be resurrected. While dead it does not move or speak.

Follow distance: the companion keeps a configurable distance behind the
leader. The chase preference (0, 1 or 2) selects close, medium or far
within a fixed safe band; the concrete distance depends on the
companion's declared profile, which the planner does not see.

Expression: the companion occasionally says a short allowlisted line.
Slots 0 and 1 are the two lines available to its profile. Expression
is cosmetic and rate limited.

Held: a held companion stops moving and attacking and keeps its last
position. Follow and offensive actions must not be proposed while held.
