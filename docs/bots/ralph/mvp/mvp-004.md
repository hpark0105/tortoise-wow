## Evidence plan

Use one disposable, port-free lab seeded with a single owned level-1 bot and
one deterministic hostile creature row from the existing `creature_template`
catalog at the bot's map and coordinates. Capture the bot's `characters.xp`
and `level` before world start, let the normal `PlayerBotAI` combat loop select
and kill the creature, then query the same row after the death credit appears.
The test must identify the bot by GUID and name, require a positive XP delta,
and reject any scenario that writes XP directly or uses a GM command. Keep the
fixture config-gated and remove its compose project and volumes on every exit.

The first draft of this fixture logged the owned bot in but produced no XP
delta; the seeded creature was not acquired by the normal target loop. The
next attempt must verify creature-template faction, active-grid loading, and
spawn health before treating the combat path as ready.

Use a disposable, port-free Docker project. Do not deploy or modify the personal database volume. The hosted head independently reviews source, diff, tests and acceptance.

An active-spawn retry also failed closed with XP `0 -> 0`; the bot login was
healthy but no combat credit was observed. The fixture needs a server-supported
deterministic combat probe or a verified loaded spawn before acceptance.
